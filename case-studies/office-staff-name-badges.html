Project need

The buying problem behind this order.

The buyer wanted a professional metal name tag program that could support branch staff, name changes and future reorder quantities without inconsistent layout.

The quote process focused on artwork clarity, size, material, finish, mounting method, packing, MOQ and production timing. These are the details most B2B buyers should prepare before sampling.

  • Product type: custom office name badges and engraved metal name tags.
  • Material: stainless steel, aluminum, brass or plated zinc alloy.
  • Finish: brushed silver, brushed gold, black coating, engraving or printed logo.
  • Backing: magnetic back, pin back, clip or adhesive backing.
  • MOQ: typical starting quantity is 100 pieces per design.
  • Timing: about 7 days for sample and about 15 days for bulk production after approval.

Key decisions before sampling.

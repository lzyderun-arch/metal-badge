OEM machinery plate project

Machinery nameplates with model data, serial numbers and mounting holes.

Project need

The buying problem behind this order.

The buyer needed the plate to stay readable after installation and carry technical data accurately across repeated equipment batches.

The quote process focused on artwork clarity, size, material, finish, mounting method, packing, MOQ and production timing. These are the details most B2B buyers should prepare before sampling.

  • Product type: machinery nameplates and OEM equipment plates.
  • Material: stainless steel, aluminum, brass or plated metal.
  • Fields: model number, voltage, serial number, warning text and QR code.
  • Mounting: screw holes, rivets, 3M adhesive backing or custom hole layout.
  • MOQ: typical starting quantity is 100 pieces per design.
  • Documents: ISO and RoHS documents can be reviewed for qualified B2B orders.

Manufacturing decisions

Key decisions before sampling.

These decisions affect price, sample accuracy and repeat production consistency.

Technical Layout

Fixed and variable fields were separated so serial data could change without shifting the design.

Hole Drawing

Hole diameter, spacing and corner radius were checked before sampling.

Surface Finish

Engraving, printing and brushed finish options were compared for readability and durability.

FAQ

Machinery Nameplates case questions.

Can machinery nameplates include technical data?

Yes. Model, voltage, input, serial number, QR code and caution text can be included.

Is a drawing required?

A drawing is strongly recommended when the plate has exact holes, rounded corners or adhesive backing.

Can serial numbers change across a batch?

Yes. Variable serial data can be produced from an Excel file.

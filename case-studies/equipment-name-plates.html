Project requirements

Industrial name plates need readable data and correct mounting.

The plate layout included model name, voltage data, serial number and safety text. During quote review, we confirmed material, plate thickness, hole position and adhesive backing because installation requirements affect production.

  • Product type: equipment name plates and engraved metal tags.
  • Material: stainless steel, aluminum, brass or plated metal.
  • Marking: engraving, printing, etching or stamped relief.
  • Mounting: screw holes, adhesive backing or 3M tape.
  • Data: serial numbers, QR codes, model names and caution text.

Quote focus

FAQ

Equipment name plate questions.

Can equipment name plates include serial numbers?

Yes. Serial numbers, model names, voltage data, QR codes and warning text can be engraved or printed.

Which materials work for industrial metal tags?

Stainless steel, aluminum, brass and plated metal are common choices.

Can plates include adhesive backing?

Yes. Adhesive backing, 3M tape and screw hole mounting can be quoted.

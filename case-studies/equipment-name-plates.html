Project requirements

Industrial name plates need readable data and correct mounting.

The plate layout included model name, voltage data, serial number and safety text. During quote review, we confirmed material, plate thickness, hole position and adhesive backing because installation requirements affect production.

  • Product type: equipment name plates and engraved metal tags.
  • Material: stainless steel, aluminum, brass or plated metal.
  • Marking: engraving, printing, etching or stamped relief.
  • Mounting: screw holes, adhesive backing or 3M tape.
  • Data: serial numbers, QR codes, model names and caution text.

Before pricing

What to send for an equipment nameplate quote.

Industrial buyers save time by confirming installation details before sample artwork is prepared.

Plate Drawing

Send length, width, thickness, corner radius, screw hole diameter, hole spacing and whether the plate needs rounded corners.

Data File

Send model numbers, serial numbers, QR codes, voltage fields and caution text in Excel when each plate carries unique information.

Environment

Confirm indoor, outdoor, oil, heat, abrasion or chemical exposure so stainless steel, aluminum, brass, printing or engraving can be selected properly.

Manufacturing notes

The buyer checklist behind this equipment plate order.

This type of project is closer to an equipment data plate than a decorative badge. The quote review needs fixed artwork, variable data file, hole drawing, backing requirement and packing method before sampling.

  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces, adjusted by material, process and variable data.
  • Common reference thickness: 5 mm for heavier metal pieces; thinner plates can be quoted by drawing.
  • Surface options: engraving, digital printing, raised detail, recessed detail, hot gold or silver stamping and brushed finish.
  • Accepted artwork files: PSD, PDF and editable vector files when available.
  • Certification support: ISO and RoHS documents can be reviewed for qualified B2B orders.

FAQ

Equipment name plate questions.

Can equipment name plates include serial numbers?

Yes. Serial numbers, model names, voltage data, QR codes and warning text can be engraved or printed.

Which materials work for industrial metal tags?

Stainless steel, aluminum, brass and plated metal are common choices.

Can plates include adhesive backing?

Yes. Adhesive backing, 3M tape and screw hole mounting can be quoted.

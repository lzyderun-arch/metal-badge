Project requirements

Challenge coins need weight, edge detail and clear relief.

The coin artwork included a central emblem, border text and award wording. We planned relief depth, antique finish and edge style so the coin felt substantial and readable from both sides.

  • Product type: custom challenge coins and commemorative coins.
  • Process: die struck or cast coin with 2D or 3D relief.
  • Finish: antique brass, antique silver, gold, nickel or copper.
  • Options: two-sided artwork, edge text, numbering and enamel color.
  • Packing: capsule, velvet pouch, acrylic box or presentation case.

Quote focus

Designed for awards, clubs and commemorative programs.

Coin buyers should confirm diameter, thickness, one-side or two-side artwork, finish, edge detail, numbering and presentation packaging before quoting.

Two-sided relief

Front and back artwork can carry separate emblems, dates or recognition text.

Antique texture

Antique plating improves contrast and makes raised detail easier to read.

Gift packing

Capsules, boxes and pouches improve perceived value for award programs.

FAQ

Custom challenge coin questions.

Can challenge coins be made with two-sided designs?

Yes. Two-sided relief, text, logos and serial detail can be planned during artwork review.

Which finishes work well for challenge coins?

Antique brass, antique silver, gold, nickel, copper and dual plating are common options.

Can coins be packed individually?

Yes. Capsules, velvet pouches, acrylic boxes and presentation cases are available.

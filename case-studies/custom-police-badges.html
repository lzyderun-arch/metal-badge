Security badge project

Custom police badges with raised relief, enamel color and secure backing.

A security buyer needed official-looking metal badges with crisp emblem detail, plated outlines, enamel color and reliable attachment for uniform use.

Project requirements

Badge authority depends on relief, finish and attachment.

The design used a shield profile, raised border, recessed enamel areas and polished lettering. During artwork review, we separated fine line detail from enamel zones so the badge would remain readable after plating.

  • Product type: custom police badges and security badges.
  • Process: die struck relief with enamel color fill.
  • Finish: nickel, gold or antique brass options.
  • Backing: safety pin, clutch, screw post or magnetic option.
  • Packing: individual poly bag or presentation box.

Quote focus

Useful for security teams, clubs and official uniforms.

Buyers comparing custom police badges should confirm badge size, metal thickness, backing strength and whether the badge needs retail or presentation packaging.

Artwork

Vector artwork helps preserve crest lines, lettering and shield shapes in the mold.

Finish

Antique plating creates depth, while polished nickel or gold gives a sharper official look.

Attachment

Uniform badges need stronger attachment planning than small lapel pins.

FAQ

Custom police badge questions.

What details are needed for custom police badge pricing?

Send artwork, size, quantity, plating finish, enamel color needs, backing type and packaging needs.

Can security badges use antique plating?

Yes. Antique brass, antique silver, nickel, gold and black nickel finishes are available.

Can each badge be packed separately?

Yes. Individual bags, backing cards, velvet pouches and presentation boxes can be quoted.

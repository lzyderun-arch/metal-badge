Hospitality gift project

Custom hotel keychains and engraved metal key tags for hospitality brands.

A hospitality buyer needed metal keychains that looked premium enough for guest gifts while staying durable for daily key use.

Project requirements

Hospitality keychains need finish consistency and durable hardware.

The project compared brushed metal engraving, enamel logo color and polished plated edges. Split ring strength and gift-ready packing were included in the quote because the product would be handled daily.

  • Product type: custom hotel keychains and metal key tags.
  • Material: zinc alloy, stainless steel, brass or iron.
  • Decoration: engraving, enamel color, printed logo or cutout shape.
  • Hardware: split ring, chain, swivel hook or bottle opener design.
  • Packing: individual bag, backing card or gift box.

Quote focus

Useful for hotels, real estate teams and brand gifts.

Custom keychain buyers should confirm whether the design is a daily-use item, a promotional gift or a retail product because material thickness and packaging change by use case.

Engraving

Clean line artwork works well on brushed steel, plated zinc alloy and brass tags.

Enamel logo

Brand color can be added where the keychain needs stronger visual identity.

Retail packing

Backing cards and boxes help hotel and gift shop programs look finished.

FAQ

Custom hotel keychain questions.

Can hotel keychains be engraved?

Yes. Logos, room series and brand marks can be engraved, stamped or enamel filled.

Can keychains include bottle opener functions?

Yes. Bottle opener keychains can be made with brushed metal, plating and enamel logos.

What hardware options are available?

Split rings, chains, swivel hooks and custom connectors can be quoted.

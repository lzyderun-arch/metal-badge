Asset tag project

QR code metal tags for asset tracking and equipment traceability.

An equipment buyer needed scannable QR code metal tags that could carry unique serial data, survive handling and mount securely to assets and machinery.

Project need

The buying problem behind this order.

The project required variable QR data, readable codes, durable marking and mounting options that would not fail during regular equipment use.

The quote process focused on artwork clarity, size, material, finish, mounting method, packing, MOQ and production timing. These are the details most B2B buyers should prepare before sampling.

  • Product type: custom QR code metal tags and engraved asset tags.
  • Material: stainless steel, aluminum, brass or coated metal.
  • Marking: laser engraving, digital printing, etching or black fill.
  • Mounting: screw holes, rivet holes, slots, 3M adhesive or custom layouts.
  • Data: QR codes, serial numbers, barcode fields and asset names.
  • Timing: about 7 days for sample and about 15 days for bulk production after approval.

Manufacturing decisions

Key decisions before sampling.

These decisions affect price, sample accuracy and repeat production consistency.

QR Readability

Code size and contrast were checked before production to reduce scanning problems.

Variable Data

The buyer supplied QR links and serial numbers in spreadsheet format.

Mounting Method

Hole layout and adhesive backing were reviewed based on where the tags would be installed.

FAQ

QR Code Metal Tags case questions.

Can every tag use a different QR code?

Yes. Variable QR codes and serial numbers can be produced from an Excel or CSV file.

Can QR codes be engraved?

Yes. Engraving is common for durable asset tags, while printing can improve contrast for some designs.

What should be tested before approval?

QR readability, code size, contrast and mounting location should be checked before bulk production.

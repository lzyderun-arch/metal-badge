Process comparison

Soft enamel vs hard enamel pins.

Both processes create durable custom metal pins, but the surface, visual depth and finishing steps are different. The right choice depends on artwork, presentation and use.

Request Pin PricingView Metal Pins

Soft enamel has a textured surface

In a soft enamel pin, enamel color sits below the raised metal borders. The relief can be felt when the surface is touched. This process is useful when the buyer wants visible metal outlines, strong color separation and a dimensional appearance.

Hard enamel has a smooth polished surface

Hard enamel color is filled and polished level with the metal lines. The finished pin feels smooth and has a more uniform reflective surface. It is often selected for corporate programs, premium collections and projects where a polished presentation is the priority.

Material, size and finish planning

Zinc alloy is a common choice for custom shapes and dimensional details. A 40 mm pin is a useful reference size, but wearable products should be balanced for weight and attachment strength. Gold-tone and silver-tone plating, digital print details, raised metal and recessed areas can be reviewed during artwork preparation.

MOQ and production schedule

The typical starting MOQ is 100 pieces per design and can change with the process. A sample normally takes about 7 days after artwork confirmation. Bulk production normally takes about 15 days after sample approval. More complex shapes, color work or packing can affect timing.

Which process should you choose?

Choose soft enamel when tactile relief and visible metal outlines support the design. Choose hard enamel when a smooth polished surface matters more. Send a PSD, PDF or available artwork file if you are unsure; the design can be reviewed before a process is recommended.

FAQ

Enamel pin comparison questions.

Which one feels smooth?

Hard enamel is polished level with the metal borders. Soft enamel retains recessed color areas and a textured feel.

Can both use custom shapes?

Yes. Shape, cutouts and detail are reviewed against the selected material and mold process.

What should I send?

Send artwork, quantity, dimensions, finish, attachment and delivery requirements for review.

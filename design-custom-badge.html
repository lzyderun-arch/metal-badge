FAQ

Custom metal badge configurator questions.

Use the planning range as a starting point, then confirm final details with the production team.

Can I start without finished artwork?

Yes. A sketch, logo or reference image is enough to begin. The team will confirm whether the design needs die striking, casting, enamel or printed color.

What quantity should I enter?

Enter the expected production quantity, with 100 pieces as the standard starting point. Larger quantities often reduce the unit cost after tooling.

Is the range a final quote?

No. It is a planning range to help compare options. A final quote follows artwork, tooling, sample approval, packaging and freight review.

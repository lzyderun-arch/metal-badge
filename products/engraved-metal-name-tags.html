Personalization

Plan the name, title and logo before production.

Engraved metal name tags need clear text hierarchy. The logo should be readable at small size, the employee name should have enough space, and the title or department line should not crowd the backing area. If the order includes many names, send an Excel file so each badge can be checked against the same template.

For hotel and office programs, confirm whether the badge should look brushed silver, brushed gold, black coated or polished. The finish changes contrast and determines whether the logo should be engraved, printed or raised.

  • Accepted artwork: PSD, PDF and editable vector files when available.
  • Material choices: stainless steel, aluminum, brass or plated zinc alloy.
  • Name data: individual name, title, department and branch fields.
  • Backings: magnetic, pin, clip or adhesive backing.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design.
  • Lead time: about 7 days for sample and about 15 days for bulk production.

FAQ

Engraved metal name tag questions.

What files are needed for a quote?

Send logo artwork, badge size, quantity, backing preference and a spreadsheet of individual names if the order is personalized.

Can the logo be printed in color?

Yes. Logos can be engraved, printed in color, recessed, raised or combined with brushed metal depending on the brand requirement.

Can reorder names match the first batch?

Yes. Keep the approved template and provide new name data for repeat production.

FAQ

Soft enamel pin questions.

Common decisions before starting a soft enamel pin order.

What makes soft enamel pins different?

Soft enamel pins use raised metal borders with recessed enamel color. The surface has a textured feel and works well for detailed artwork.

What is the typical MOQ and lead time for soft enamel pins?

The typical starting MOQ is 100 pieces per design and can change with the process. Sampling normally takes about 7 days, followed by about 15 days for bulk production after approval.

Which plating finishes work for soft enamel pins?

Common options include gold, nickel, black nickel, antique brass, antique silver, copper and matte finishes.

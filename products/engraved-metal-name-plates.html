FAQ

Engraved name plate questions.

Common details for OEM metal plate production.

Which materials work for engraved metal name plates?

Stainless steel, aluminum, brass and plated metal are common choices depending on the environment, finish and mounting requirements.

Can equipment tags include serial numbers?

Yes. Serial numbers, model numbers, warning text, logos and data fields can be engraved, printed or etched.

What mounting options are available?

Options include adhesive backing, screw holes, rivet holes, rounded corners and custom shapes.

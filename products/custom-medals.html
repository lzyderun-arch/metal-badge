FAQ

Custom medal questions.

Common details for event medal production.

Can custom medals include ribbons?

Yes. Medals can be supplied with custom ribbon colors, printed ribbon designs, jump rings and event-ready packing.

Which finishes are common for award medals?

Gold, silver, bronze, antique gold, antique silver and dual plating are common choices for award medals.

Can race medals use custom shapes?

Yes. Race medals and event medallions can use round, shield, star or fully custom outlines based on artwork.

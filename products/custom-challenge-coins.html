Specification planning

Confirm the details that change cost, sample accuracy and production timing.

Challenge coins need clear front and back hierarchy. Raised borders, recessed backgrounds and antique plating help the artwork stay visible without relying on too much color.

  • Common material: zinc alloy for dimensional relief, custom edge detail and thicker coin profiles.
  • Common size: 40 mm can work for compact coins; larger diameters improve text readability.
  • Surface options: antique gold, antique silver, antique copper, bright plating, enamel color and digital printing.
  • Edge options: flat edge, rope edge, reeded edge or custom rim depending on artwork.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design, adjusted by two-sided tooling and finish.
  • Sample time is about 7 days; bulk production is about 15 days after sample approval.

FAQ

Custom Challenge Coins questions.

Common manufacturing questions before sending artwork for review.

Can challenge coins be two-sided?

Yes. Front and back artwork can both include raised relief, recessed texture, enamel color and plated metal detail.

Which finish is best for challenge coins?

Antique gold, antique silver and antique copper are common because they make relief detail easier to read.

Can custom challenge coins include packaging?

Yes. Velvet pouches, coin capsules, presentation boxes and retail cards can be quoted with the order.

FAQ

Custom keychain questions.

Common options for metal keychain orders.

What types of custom metal keychains are available?

Options include logo key rings, hotel key tags, bottle opener keychains, enamel keychains and engraved promotional keychains.

Can metal keychains include enamel color?

Yes. Soft enamel, hard enamel, printed logo and engraved details can be used depending on the design and budget.

What attachments are available for keychains?

Split rings, chain links, lobster clasps, ball chains and custom connector hardware are available.

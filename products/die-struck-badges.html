Raised relief

Classic metal detail for logos, emblems and official badges.

Die struck construction is best when the badge needs sharp raised metal detail, antique contrast and a premium no-enamel look.

  • Zinc alloy is a common material for custom relief and shapes.
  • Gold-tone, silver-tone, raised and recessed details can be reviewed.
  • Attachment is selected according to size, weight and intended use.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design, subject to process.
  • About 7 days for a sample and about 15 days for bulk production after approval.

FAQ

Die struck badge questions.

Artwork and finish details for relief badge projects.

What artwork works best for die struck badges?

Clean vector artwork with strong outlines, readable text and clear relief areas works best for die struck badges.

Can die struck badges be made without enamel?

Yes. Many die struck badges use plated metal only, antique finish and polished raised edges without enamel color.

Which uses fit die struck badges?

Die struck badges fit official emblems, club badges, recognition pins, uniform badges and premium metal badge logos.

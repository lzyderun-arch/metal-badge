Specification planning

Confirm the details that change cost, sample accuracy and production timing.

A logo badge can be worn, attached to packaging or fixed to a product. The same artwork may need different backings, thickness or plating depending on where the badge will be used.

  • Common material: zinc alloy for custom outlines, relief and shaped logo badges.
  • Common size: 40 mm is a useful starting point; final dimensions depend on logo detail.
  • Surface options: gold plating, silver plating, digital printing, raised logo, recessed color and antique finish.
  • Backings: clutch, safety pin, magnet, screw post, adhesive backing or custom fixture.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design, adjusted by mold and packing requirements.
  • Sample time is about 7 days; bulk production is about 15 days after approval.

FAQ

Custom Logo Metal Badges questions.

Common manufacturing questions before sending artwork for review.

Can logo metal badges match brand colors?

Yes. Enamel and printed colors can be matched during artwork review and confirmed during sample approval.

Which backing should I choose for a logo badge?

For clothing, clutch, safety pin or magnet are common. For packaging or hard goods, adhesive backing, screw post or custom fixture may be better.

Can logo badges be made in custom shapes?

Yes. Custom outlines, cutouts, raised borders and recessed details can be quoted from your artwork.

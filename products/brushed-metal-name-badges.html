Uniform programs

Built for repeat staff name badge orders.

Brushed metal name badges are a practical choice for hotels, office teams, restaurants, retail stores, property management companies and distributor programs. The brushed surface reduces glare, keeps the badge professional and works well with engraved names or printed color logos.

For repeat orders, keep one approved template for logo position, name field, title line, backing and finish. New staff names can then be produced from a spreadsheet without rebuilding the full artwork each time.

  • Common materials: stainless steel, aluminum, brass or plated zinc alloy.
  • Finish options: brushed silver, brushed gold, black coating, printed logo or engraved text.
  • Backings: magnetic back, pin back, clip or adhesive backing.
  • Typical MOQ: 100 pieces per design, adjusted by process.
  • Sample lead time: about 7 days after artwork approval.
  • Bulk production: about 15 days after sample approval.

FAQ

Brushed metal name badge questions.

Can brushed metal name badges use individual names?

Yes. Send a spreadsheet with employee names, titles and departments. The production proof can be checked before engraving or printing.

Which backing is best for uniforms?

Magnetic backing is common because it avoids holes in clothing. Pin, clip and adhesive backs can also be quoted.

Can repeat orders match previous badges?

Yes. Approved artwork, finish, backing and name layout can be kept as a reorder template for future staff additions.

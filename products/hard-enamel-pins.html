FAQ

Hard enamel pin questions.

Key choices for premium enamel pin orders.

When should I choose hard enamel pins?

Choose hard enamel when you want a smooth polished surface, premium feel and durable color for corporate lapel pins, retail pins or collectible pieces.

Are hard enamel pins more polished than soft enamel pins?

Yes. Hard enamel is filled and polished level with the metal lines, creating a flatter and smoother finish than soft enamel.

Can hard enamel pins use custom shapes?

Yes. Hard enamel pins can use custom outlines, cutouts and plated metal borders based on your vector artwork.

Specification planning

Confirm the details that change cost, sample accuracy and production timing.

Lapel pins are small, so artwork simplification matters. The best results come from clear metal borders, controlled color count and backing choices matched to clothing use.

  • Common material: zinc alloy, brass or iron depending on shape, detail and finish.
  • Common size: 25 to 40 mm; 40 mm allows stronger detail for event or retail pins.
  • Surface options: soft enamel, hard enamel, die struck, gold plating, silver plating and digital printing.
  • Backings: butterfly clutch, rubber clutch, locking clutch, magnet, safety pin or double post.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design, adjusted by color count and packaging.
  • Sample time is about 7 days; bulk production is about 15 days after approval.

FAQ

Custom Lapel Pins questions.

Common manufacturing questions before sending artwork for review.

What is the best process for logo lapel pins?

Soft enamel is common for colorful logo pins, hard enamel gives a smoother premium surface, and die struck metal works well for simple official designs.

Can lapel pins be supplied with backing cards?

Yes. Backing cards, individual bags and retail packaging can be planned during quote review.

Which file type is best for custom lapel pins?

PSD, PDF and vector artwork can be reviewed. Vector files are preferred when the artwork includes small text or detailed outlines.

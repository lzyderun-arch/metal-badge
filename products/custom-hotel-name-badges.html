FAQ

Custom Hotel Name Badges questions.

Can hotel name badges include each employee name?

Yes. Send an Excel sheet with employee names, departments and titles. The proof can be checked before engraving or printing.

Which backing is best for hotel uniforms?

Magnetic backing is common because it avoids holes in shirts and jackets. Pin and clip backs can also be quoted.

Can reorder batches match the first order?

Yes. Logo position, finish, backing and name layout can be kept as a reorder template for future staff additions.

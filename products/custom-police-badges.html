Specification planning

Confirm the details that change cost, sample accuracy and production timing.

Police-style badges need to feel authoritative, readable and durable on uniforms. The most important decisions are badge size, shield outline, relief depth, plating contrast, enamel placement and attachment strength.

  • Common material: zinc alloy for custom shield shapes, deep relief and durable uniform badges.
  • Common size: 40 mm is a practical starting reference; thickness around 5 mm can be reviewed by design weight.
  • Surface options: gold plating, silver plating, antique brass, black nickel, raised lettering, recessed details and selective enamel.
  • Backings: safety pin, screw post, clutch, magnet or stronger custom attachment depending on uniform use.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design, adjusted by mold complexity and finish.
  • Sample time is about 7 days after artwork confirmation; bulk production is about 15 days after sample approval.

FAQ

Custom Police Badges questions.

Common manufacturing questions before sending artwork for review.

What details are needed for custom police badge pricing?

Send artwork, size, quantity, plating finish, enamel color needs, backing type and packing requirements. Reference photos help clarify the badge style.

Can custom police badges use antique plating?

Yes. Antique brass, antique silver and antique copper can make raised relief more visible, while polished gold or nickel creates a brighter official look.

What backing is suitable for security badges?

Safety pin, screw post, clutch, magnet and stronger custom attachments can be reviewed according to badge size, weight and uniform use.

Specification planning

Confirm the details that change cost, sample accuracy and production timing.

Award medals need clear ranking, consistent repeat production and packaging that matches the value of the recognition program.

  • Common material: zinc alloy for custom shapes, raised logos and durable medallions.
  • Common size: 40 mm for compact medals; larger medals can improve perceived value.
  • Surface options: gold, silver, bronze, antique plating, digital printing, raised and recessed details.
  • Accessories: ribbon, jump ring, velvet pouch, presentation box or award card.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design, adjusted by finish and packing.
  • Sample time is about 7 days; bulk production is about 15 days after approval.

FAQ

Custom Award Medals questions.

Common manufacturing questions before sending artwork for review.

Can award medals use the same mold for different levels?

Yes. One medal mold can often be used with gold, silver and bronze finishes to control tooling cost.

Can medals be packed in boxes?

Yes. Velvet pouches, presentation boxes, backing cards and individual bags can be quoted.

What should I send for an award medal quote?

Send artwork, size, quantity by award level, ribbon requirements, finish preference and packing details.

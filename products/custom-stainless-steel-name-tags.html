Can stainless steel name tags include QR codes?

Yes. QR codes, serial numbers and model fields can be engraved or printed from supplied data.

Can stainless steel tags use magnetic backing?

Yes for uniform badges. Equipment tags usually use holes, rivets or adhesive backing.

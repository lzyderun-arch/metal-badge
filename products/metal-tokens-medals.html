Tokens and medals

Custom metal pieces for events, games and award programs.

Metal token coins and custom metal tokens are related to custom metal coins, but buyers often have different needs: game pieces, payment tokens, event access tokens, medals or medallions.

  • Round, square, shield, star and fully custom token shapes.
  • Raised logo, recessed serial number, QR code area or enamel color zones.
  • Ribbon, jump ring, keychain, pin back or no attachment.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design, subject to process.
  • Lead time: about 7 days for a sample and about 15 days for bulk production after approval.
  • Packing requirements are confirmed for each project.

FAQ

Custom token and medal questions.

Common choices for event tokens, game pieces and award medal orders.

What is the difference between metal tokens and medals?

Metal tokens are often used as game pieces, payment tokens, event access tokens or collectible items. Medals usually include award messaging and may use a ribbon, medal box or presentation card.

Can custom metal tokens include serial numbers or QR code areas?

Yes. Tokens can include raised logos, recessed serial numbers, QR code areas, enamel color zones or plain areas for later marking.

What is the typical MOQ and lead time for custom medals?

The typical starting MOQ is 100 pieces per design and can change with the process. Sampling normally takes about 7 days, followed by about 15 days for bulk production after approval.

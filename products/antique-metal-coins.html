Specification planning

Confirm the details that change cost, sample accuracy and production timing.

Antique coins rely on contrast between raised metal and recessed dark areas. This finish is useful when artwork includes crests, numbers, wreaths, shields or commemorative text.

  • Common material: zinc alloy for dimensional coin relief and custom edge profiles.
  • Common size: 40 mm for compact coin programs; larger coins allow more readable rim text.
  • Surface options: antique gold, antique silver, antique copper, black nickel accents and enamel color.
  • Artwork options: two-sided relief, rim lettering, raised numbers, recessed texture and custom edge.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design, adjusted by two-sided tooling and packaging.
  • Sample time is about 7 days; bulk production is about 15 days after approval.

FAQ

Antique Metal Coins questions.

Common manufacturing questions before sending artwork for review.

What is an antique metal coin finish?

Antique plating darkens recessed areas and leaves raised relief brighter, making artwork details more visible.

Can antique coins include enamel color?

Yes. Enamel can be added selectively to logos, flags, icons or important color areas.

Are antique coins suitable for two-sided artwork?

Yes. Two-sided antique coins are common for challenge coins, commemorative coins and recognition programs.

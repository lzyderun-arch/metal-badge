Asset identification

Built around quote-ready production details.

Custom Equipment Tags should be specified around actual use: artwork, size, material, finish, attachment, quantity, packing and whether each piece uses different text or serial data. Clear details reduce quoting time and help the first sample match the bulk order.

BadgeCraft Metalworks supports OEM production for North America, Canada and Europe orders. The usual starting MOQ is 100 pieces per design, sample time is about 7 days after artwork approval, and bulk production is about 15 days after sample confirmation.

  • Common materials: stainless steel, aluminum, brass or plated metal.
  • Surface options: engraving, digital printing, etching, brushed finish or recessed panels.
  • Attachment or mounting: screw holes, rivet holes, 3M adhesive, slots or ball chain.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design, adjusted by process.
  • Sample lead time: about 7 days after artwork approval.
  • Bulk production: about 15 days after sample approval.
  • Accepted artwork files: PSD, PDF and editable vector files when available.

FAQ

Custom Equipment Tags questions.

Can equipment tags include different serial numbers?

Yes. Variable serial numbers, QR codes and model fields can be produced from an Excel file.

Which material is best for equipment tags?

Stainless steel and aluminum are common. Brass or plated metal can be used when appearance is more important.

Can tags include holes or adhesive backing?

Yes. Screw holes, rivet holes, slots, 3M tape and adhesive backing can be quoted from a drawing.

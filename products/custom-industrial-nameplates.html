Specification planning

Confirm the details that change cost, sample accuracy and production timing.

Industrial nameplates need more than a logo. Buyers should confirm data fields, mounting method, environment, surface durability and whether serial numbers change across the order. Equipment data plates should also define fixed fields, variable fields, warning text and whether the final plate needs ISO or RoHS support documents.

  • Common material: stainless steel, aluminum, brass or zinc alloy depending on application.
  • Common size: 40 mm can be used for compact tags; custom equipment plates are quoted by drawing.
  • Surface options: engraving, digital printing, raised text, recessed panels, brushed finish, hot gold or silver stamping and black fill.
  • Mounting: screw holes, adhesive backing, rivet holes, ball chain or custom slot layouts.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design, adjusted by variable data and material.
  • Sample time is about 7 days; bulk production is about 15 days after approval.

FAQ

Custom Industrial Nameplates questions.

Common manufacturing questions before sending artwork for review.

Can industrial nameplates include serial numbers?

Yes. Fixed artwork and variable serial numbers can be arranged if the data file is supplied clearly.

What mounting options are available?

Screw holes, rivet holes, adhesive backing, slots and custom mounting layouts can be reviewed from the drawing.

Can metal nameplates use digital printing?

Yes. Digital printing, engraving, recessed panels and raised details can be used depending on durability requirements.

Industrial identification

Specify the plate around how it will be installed.

Equipment nameplates need practical production details before quoting: plate size, thickness, data fields, material, mounting method, surface environment and whether each plate carries unique serial data. These decisions affect artwork layout, process choice, durability and packing.

For machinery labels and OEM data plates, send a drawing if screw holes, rounded corners, adhesive backing or exact hole positions are required. Serial numbers, QR codes and model fields can be prepared from spreadsheet data.

  • Materials: stainless steel, aluminum, brass or plated metal.
  • Processes: engraving, digital printing, etching, raised detail or recessed detail.
  • Mounting: screw holes, rivet holes, slots, 3M tape or adhesive backing.
  • Data: model number, serial number, warning text, QR code and voltage fields.
  • Typical MOQ: 100 pieces, adjusted by material, process and variable data.
  • Lead time: about 7 days for sample and about 15 days for bulk production.

FAQ

Equipment nameplate questions.

Can equipment nameplates include serial numbers and QR codes?

Yes. Serial numbers, model fields, warning text, barcodes and QR codes can be engraved, printed or etched from supplied data.

Which mounting options are available?

Common mounting choices include screw holes, rivet holes, slots, rounded corners, 3M adhesive backing and custom hole layouts.

What materials work for equipment nameplates?

Stainless steel, aluminum, brass and plated metal are common choices depending on environment, durability needs and budget.

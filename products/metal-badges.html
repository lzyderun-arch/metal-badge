Metal badge buyers often compare custom metal badges, metal pin badges and personalized metal badges. We separate the design into mold shape, relief level, plating, color, attachment and packaging so you can quote accurately.

FAQ

Custom metal badge questions.

Key details for preparing artwork and requesting a clear badge quote.

What information is needed for a custom metal badge quote?

Send artwork, target size, quantity, plating finish, enamel color requirements, backing type and packaging needs. Reference photos are useful if the final process is not decided.

Which backings are available for metal badges?

Common backing options include butterfly clutch, safety pin, magnetic backing, screw post and adhesive back. The best choice depends on uniform use, product size and how the badge will be attached.

Can custom metal badges include enamel colors?

Yes. Soft enamel and hard enamel are available, along with plated metal-only, antique and polished finishes. Enamel colors are matched during artwork and sample approval.

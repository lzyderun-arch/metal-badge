OEM machinery plates

Built around quote-ready production details.

Custom Machinery Nameplates should be specified around actual use: artwork, size, material, finish, attachment, quantity, packing and whether each piece uses different text or serial data. Clear details reduce quoting time and help the first sample match the bulk order.

BadgeCraft Metalworks supports OEM production for North America, Canada and Europe orders. The usual starting MOQ is 100 pieces per design, sample time is about 7 days after artwork approval, and bulk production is about 15 days after sample confirmation.

  • Common materials: stainless steel, aluminum, brass or plated metal.
  • Surface options: engraved text, printed black panels, brushed finish, recessed fields or raised details.
  • Attachment or mounting: screw holes, rivet holes, 3M adhesive backing or custom mounting.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design, adjusted by process.
  • Sample lead time: about 7 days after artwork approval.
  • Bulk production: about 15 days after sample approval.
  • Accepted artwork files: PSD, PDF and editable vector files when available.

FAQ

Custom Machinery Nameplates questions.

Can machinery nameplates include technical data fields?

Yes. Model, voltage, input, serial number, QR code and caution text can be included.

Do you need a drawing before quoting?

A drawing is recommended when the plate has holes, exact dimensions, rounded corners or adhesive backing.

Can the same layout use different serial numbers?

Yes. Supply the variable data in Excel and the layout can be prepared as a production template.

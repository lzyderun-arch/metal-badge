Specification planning

Confirm the details that change cost, sample accuracy and production timing.

Sports medal buyers usually need dependable repeat production, readable event artwork and packing that helps volunteers distribute medals quickly.

  • Common material: zinc alloy for custom shapes, raised athletes, logos and event landmarks.
  • Common size: 40 mm is suitable for small award programs; larger race medals can be quoted by artwork.
  • Surface options: gold, silver, bronze, antique finish, digital printing, raised text and recessed background texture.
  • Ribbon options: plain color, printed ribbon, sewn loop, jump ring and event-specific sorting.
  • Typical starting MOQ: 100 pieces per design, adjusted by size, ribbon and packaging.
  • Sample time is about 7 days; bulk production is about 15 days after approval.

FAQ

Custom Sports Medals questions.

Common manufacturing questions before sending artwork for review.

Can one sports medal design use gold, silver and bronze finishes?

Yes. Many award programs use one mold with different plating finishes for first, second and third place medals.

Can ribbons be customized?

Yes. Ribbon color, width, printed logo and attachment style can be quoted with the medal order.

How should race medals be packed?

Medals can be packed individually, by award level, by event category or in bulk cartons with clear labels.

FAQ

Custom metal pin questions.

Common decisions for enamel pins, lapel pins and retail pin orders.

What is the difference between soft enamel and hard enamel pins?

Soft enamel pins have raised metal lines and recessed enamel color. Hard enamel pins are polished smooth with enamel filled level to the metal surface for a more premium feel.

Which backing is common for custom metal pins?

Butterfly clutch is the most common option for many metal pin orders. Larger or premium lapel pins can use rubber clutch, locking pin back, magnet, safety pin or double-post backing.

What is the typical MOQ and lead time for custom metal pins?

The typical starting MOQ is 100 pieces per design and can change with the process. Sampling normally takes about 7 days, followed by about 15 days for bulk production after approval.

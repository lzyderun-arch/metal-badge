Built for commemorative coins, tokens and branded awards.

BadgeCraft Metalworks manufactures custom metal coins for events, recognition programs, collectible campaigns, games and corporate gifts.

  • Coin sizes from small retail tokens to oversized commemorative coins.

FAQ

Custom metal coin questions.

Details that help define relief, edge style and presentation packaging.

What artwork is needed for custom metal coins?

Send front and back artwork, target size, quantity, plating preference, relief style, edge detail and packaging requirements. If the coin needs 3D relief, reference images help define the sculpted areas.

Can custom metal coins have two-sided designs?

Yes. Custom metal coins commonly use one side for the main logo or emblem and the other side for text, date, serial number or event message.

Which finishes are available for metal coins?

Common finishes include antique gold, antique silver, polished nickel, copper and dual plating. The finish can be matched to the artwork style and intended presentation.

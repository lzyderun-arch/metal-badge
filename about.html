What we manufacture.

BadgeCraft Metalworks produces custom metal badges, pins, coins, name tags, tokens and medals with artwork review, mold proofing, finishing and inspection. Our approximately 10-person team works with 10 core production machines.

  • Bulk production: about 15 days after sample approval.
